# Flag Quest

A family trivia game for learning the flags of 100 countries, along with their capitals, landmarks and fun facts.

**Play:** https://indiandoctor.github.io/Flag-Quest/

- 100 flags in Easy (1 pt), Medium (2 pts) and Hard (3 pts) levels, mixed or played in order
- Take-turns or buzzer-race mode for up to 8 players, with high scores saved in the browser
- After each answer, photos of the capital, the landmark and the fun fact
- Then a world map (Equal Earth projection) highlighting where the country is
- Works offline once loaded: everything is in one `index.html`

Flag images come from [flag-icons](https://github.com/lipis/flag-icons) (MIT). Photos come from Wikipedia and Wikimedia Commons under their free licences.
